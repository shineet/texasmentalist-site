Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Shine, The Mentalist ("Shine," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit texasmentalist.com or contact us about booking a show, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you reach out to us — through our website, by phone, by text message — we may collect:

  • Your name and contact information (email address and phone number)
  • Event details you provide, such as event type, date, venue, and guest count
  • Any other information you choose to share with us to help plan your event

We do not collect payment card information, government identification, or other sensitive personal data through our website.

Our website may also automatically collect basic visit information (such as pages viewed) through standard website hosting analytics. We do not use this information to identify you personally, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and discuss your event
  • Send you pricing information, contracts, and event questionnaires
  • Communicate with you by email or text message (SMS) about your booking
  • Plan and personalize the entertainment for your event

Text Messaging (SMS) / SMS Communications

Users may opt in to receive SMS messages from Shine The Mentalist by providing their mobile number and actively checking the SMS consent checkbox on our contact form.

SMS messages may include responses to booking inquiries, scheduling updates, booking confirmations, reminders, contracts, and customer support communications.

Message frequency varies.

Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

Reply HELP for assistance.

SMS consent is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate our booking process (such as our scheduling and communication tools)
  • Third-party lead platforms (such as Bark or GigSalad), if that is how you originally contacted us, solely to confirm and fulfill your inquiry
  • Legal authorities, if required by law

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to plan and complete your event, and afterward for reasonable record-keeping and customer service purposes.

Your Choices

You may ask us to update or delete your information at any time by contacting us using the information below. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults planning events. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
