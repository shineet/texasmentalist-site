How Much Does a Corporate Mentalist or Magician Cost in Texas? A 2026 Pricing Guide

By Shine Thankappan

If you've never booked entertainment for a corporate event before, the pricing can feel like a black box. Here's a straight answer, based on what I actually see in the Texas market.

The general range

Corporate magicians and mentalists in Texas typically run $500 to $5,000+ per event, depending on format, length, and audience size. Close-up magic for a cocktail hour sits at the low end. A full interactive stage show for 200+ people, built around your company and your message, sits higher.

What actually moves the price:

  1. Format. Strolling and close-up magic (walking table to table) is priced differently than a stage show where everyone watches the same performance at once.
  2. Length. A 20-minute feature costs less than a full-evening presence.
  3. Audience size. Interactive mentalism for 500 people takes more preparation and more room-reading than a show for 30.
  4. Customization. A show built around your company name, product launch, or specific message takes more prep than a stock routine, and should cost accordingly.
  5. Travel. If you're outside a performer's home city, factor in travel.

What's a red flag

Extremely low quotes (under $300) for a real corporate audience usually mean you're getting a hobbyist, not someone who does this professionally, insured, and reliably. Corporate events have zero room for a no-show or an unpolished act in front of your executives and clients.

How to budget it

If entertainment is part of a larger event, a reasonable rule of thumb is 5 to 10% of total event spend — more if entertainment is the centerpiece of the evening (a holiday party or gala) rather than a side element (a conference break).
