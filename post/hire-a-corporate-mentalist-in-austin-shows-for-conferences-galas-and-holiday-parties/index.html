Hire a Corporate Mentalist in Austin: Shows for Conferences, Galas, and Holiday Parties

By Shine Thankappan

Interactive mentalism for conferences, holiday parties, galas, and client events across Austin and Central Texas. Clean, professional, and impossible to forget. With hundreds of five-star Google reviews, Shine, The Mentalist is trusted by corporate clients, event planners, and destination management companies throughout Texas.

Why Companies Book Shine

Most corporate entertainment is watched. Mentalism is experienced. Your guests become part of the performance: thoughts revealed, predictions sealed before the event and coming true on stage, and moments your team will still be talking about at the next quarterly meeting.

Shine spent 20+ years as a technology executive before taking the stage, so he knows exactly what a corporate room needs: entertainment that is smart, clean, and safe in front of your executives, your clients, and your HR team.

Show Formats

Strolling Mentalism

Cocktail hours, receptions, trade shows. Shine moves group to group performing close-up mind reading and visual magic. Perfect for networking events where you need conversation starters, not a captive audience.

Stage Show

Dinners, galas, awards nights, holiday parties. A 30 to 45 minute interactive performance where your own guests star in the impossible. Scales from a 20-person leadership dinner to a 300+ person conference.

Combination Package

Strolling during cocktails, stage show after dinner. The most-booked option for holiday parties and client appreciation events. Every show can be customized with your company name, product, or event theme.

How Booking Works

  1. Tell us about your event: date, venue, guest count, and what you want the room to feel like.
  2. Get a clear quote and formal agreement. Professional invoicing and contracts that work with corporate procurement and DMCs.
  3. Show up to an unforgettable event. Shine handles the technical details and coordinates directly with your venue or planner.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the show appropriate for a professional audience? Yes. Every performance is 100% clean. No embarrassment, no offensive material, no awkward moments in front of leadership.

What do you need from the venue? Very little. Strolling sets need no setup at all. Stage shows need a sound system and a small performance area. Shine coordinates directly with your venue.

How far in advance should we book? For October through December events, book by early fall. Prime holiday dates go first. Other seasons typically need 3 to 6 weeks of lead time.

Do you work with event planners and DMCs? Regularly. Shine provides formal performance agreements, W-9s, and invoicing built for corporate procurement.

What areas do you serve? Based in the Austin area. Performs throughout Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, San Antonio, and travels across Texas and beyond.
