Services

Choose how you want the room to react

Three ways to bring interactive mentalism to your event — strolling, on stage, or both. Every format is clean, executive-safe, and customized to your night.

Format 01

Strolling Mentalism

Close-up mind reading and visual magic that moves group to group — perfect for cocktail hours, receptions, and trade-show booths where you want energy and conversation.

  • Ideal for networking and mingling
  • No stage, setup, or A/V required
  • Draws a crowd and breaks the ice
Format 02

Stage Show

A 30–45 minute interactive headline performance for dinners, galas, awards nights, and holiday parties — the centerpiece everyone talks about afterward.

  • Scales from 20-person dinners to 300+ conferences
  • Interactive: your guests drive the show
  • Impossible predictions and genuine astonishment
Format 03 · Most booked

Combination Package

Strolling during the reception, then a full stage show — the most-requested way to run a complete corporate evening, with the energy building all night.

  • Cocktail-hour strolling + headline stage set
  • One seamless entertainment arc for the night
  • Best value for full evening events

Simple to host

Strolling sets

Stage shows

Larger rooms (150+)

A raised stage and a screen or projector are helpful but not required.
