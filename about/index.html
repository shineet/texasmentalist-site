About Shine

The mind reader who used to run the meeting

Part performer, part technology executive — Shine brings 20+ years in the corporate world to a mentalism act built for exactly that room.

Shine is an Austin-based corporate mentalist and magician who spent 20+ years as a technology executive before taking his act full-time. That background is the whole point: he understands your room, your audience, and the difference between entertainment that's merely watched and an experience your guests are part of.

Across 300+ performances throughout Texas, his approach has stayed the same — mentalism where the guests become the stars. Through thought revelations, sealed predictions, and moments no one can quite explain, a roomful of colleagues turns into the story everyone's still retelling on Monday.

Every show is 100% clean and executive-safe — no crude material, no awkward volunteer moments — and it's tailored to your event, weaving in company names, products, or themes so it feels made for the night, because it is.
